# Scanner settings (Epson Perfection V500 Photo and similar CCD flatbeds)

# Epson Perfection V500 Photo: scan settings for colony formation plates

Epson Scan, Professional Mode. Document Type: Reflective. Document Source: Document Table. Auto Exposure Type: Photo. Image Type: 48-bit Color. Resolution: 600 dpi. Target Size: Original; Scaling 100%. Preview marquee: plate plus about 1 cm margin, saved with the configuration. Unsharp Mask off; Descreening off; Color Restoration off; Backlight Correction off; Dust Removal off; Grain Reduction off; Digital ICE off. Histogram: black 0, white 255, gamma 1.0, all channels. Tone Correction: Linear. Configuration \> Color: Continuous Auto Exposure off; Color Control: No Color Correction. File format: TIFF (uncompressed or LZW) with the resolution tag embedded; never JPEG. Save as a named configuration (CFA-600-linear) and reuse it for every plate.

Physical setup. Lid removed; plate bottom-down on the glass; wells fully dry. Plate centred on the bed with its long axis along the carriage direction; position marked with tape and reused for every plate (the V500 is a CCD scanner with a lens and shows rim parallax that varies with position). Opaque white cover over the plate. Kodak/Tiffen Q-13 Color Separation Guide and Gray Scale (B&H: https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/26662-REG/Kodak_1527654_Color_Separation_Guide_and.html) in every scan as an intensity reference. All plates of a comparison scanned in one session with the same configuration. File name = sample name.

After rescanning at a new resolution: re-derive the edge exclusion radius (density profile) and the OD threshold (plateau sweep), and compare with manual counts of two or three wells once.
